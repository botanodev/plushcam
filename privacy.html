PlushCam Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026

Your data

No account is needed. The photos you take and the buddies you create stay on your phone.

Permissions

Permission Used for
CameraTaking photos
Storage (Android 9 and 10 only)Saving photos to your gallery

Network

  • Background removal model: downloaded by Google Play services on Android; built into the system on iPhone
  • Purchases: payment is handled by Google Play / the App Store

Deletion

Uninstalling the app deletes all buddies. Photos saved to your gallery stay in the "PlushCam" album.

Children

This app is not designed for children under 13.
